A couple of years ago I've bought a **neopixel** led strip and enjoyed playing around with it.
I  made special animations like snake, shooting, rainbow patterns etc.
The led stip was $50\;\text{cm}$, this is not a lot of real estate to play with, I went looking further.
I found the `8x8 WS2812` led matrix, the move from 1D to 2D, an extra dimension means more animations.
This wasn't enough, I found [nanoleaf](https://nanoleaf.me) and felt in love immediately.
